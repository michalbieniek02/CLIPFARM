Scope: extend the existing Windows toolbar and VOD form. Mode: Operate.

## Direction contract

THESIS: show a release as a compact actionable update icon; download work has readable live progress.

OWN-WORLD: inherit Theme.qml colors, typography, radii, focus states and reduced-motion timing.

STORY: check in the background every five minutes; the user chooses installation, sees actual received bytes, then returns to the saved project after restart.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a 44px blue update control sits beside project actions. During download it becomes a 300px status and animated determinate bar. The VOD form wraps its live ETA and saved size above its buttons.

FORM: narrow extension of the established toolbar and form; no seed or visual-world replacement is applicable. The existing width animation connects successive real progress events.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
